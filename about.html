Hi! Here's a bit more about me:

I started my career in journalism, reporting for the San Francisco Business Times. That training shapes how I work: evidence-based storytelling and critical thinking about how content reaches and influences audiences.

I brought that approach to Workest, the content hub from Zenefits (now TriNet), growing our audience and building an editorial voice trusted by HR leaders. Today, that means pairing sharp editorial judgment with real fluency in how content gets discovered: SEO, AI search, and the workflows behind both.
