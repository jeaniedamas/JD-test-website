JD Logo — Script Options

Same "Blend" overlap concept, four cursive/script directions — from formal calligraphy to casual handwriting.

JD

Pinyon Script

Formal, flowing calligraphy with dramatic flourishes — the most elegant/traditional of the four, like an engraved monogram.

JD

Berkshire Swash

Bold, swashy, and much more legible at small sizes than a true script — reads as a confident signature rather than handwriting.

JD

Yellowtail

Retro brush-script, mid-century feel, single confident stroke weight — casual-elegant, a bit playful.

JD

Caveat

Genuine handwriting feel — the most personal and approachable, least "designed." Good if you want warmth over polish.
