JD Logo — Font Options

Same "Blend" overlap concept, four different typefaces — all contrast against the page's Schibsted Grotesk / Inter sans pairing.

JD

Playfair Display

High-contrast Didone serif — sharp, editorial, a bit formal. Also a callback to the original reference site's own display type.

JD

Fraunces

Soft, warm, slightly quirky serif with rounded ink-traps. Feels more contemporary and human than Playfair.

JD

Bebas Neue

Tall, condensed, poster-like. Boldest structural contrast to the page's wider grotesque type — reads more graphic than editorial.

JD

Instrument Serif

Elegant, thin-contrast modern serif — trendy in current tech/SaaS branding. Lighter and more delicate than the other three.
