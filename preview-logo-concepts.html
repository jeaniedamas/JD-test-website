JD Monogram Concepts

Four directions for blending the initials — pick one to refine, or mix elements across them.

J D

1. Blend

J and D overlap directly in your two brand colors, multiplied together so the overlap reads as a third, richer tone.

JD

2. Seal

A tightly kerned "JD" lockup inside a thin ring — editorial, badge-like, a quiet nod to a journalism background.

3. Ligature

A single abstract glyph: shared stem, D's bowl on top, J's hook continuing below — works small, good favicon candidate.

D J

4. Framed

A bold, faint "D" fills a rounded badge as a backdrop; a solid "J" sits forward on top — depth through layering, not color.
